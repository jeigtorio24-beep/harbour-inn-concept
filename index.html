Good to know

Before you pack.

Short answers to the questions guests ask most. Anything else: call or email the inn.

Where do I check in?
At our sister property, the Telegraph House & Motel, 479 Chebucto St. From there it's a 4–5 minute walk to the Dunlop Inn at 552 Chebucto St.
Is there a beach?
Yes. The inn has a private sandy beach and a waterside deck: Baddeck's only waterfront accommodations.
Do the rooms have private bathrooms?
Yes. Every room has a private bath (in the Spinnaker Room it is just off the hallway), and every room is air-conditioned.
Can I bring my pet?
Sorry, no pets.
Can I cook at the inn?
No, there is no cooking at the inn.
How can I pay?
Cash, Visa or Mastercard.
What are the rates, and when are you open?
Ask us: email waterside@dunlopinn.ca or call 902-295-3355 with your rough dates.
